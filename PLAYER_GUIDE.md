# Trick — Player Guide

## Make your deck

Bring **two standard decks** with matching backs. Your four Jokers are **Blessed** cards, kept in reserve. Aces are 1; Jacks, Queens, and Kings are **zero-value statuses**, six of each suit.

| Suit | Approach | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spades — Strength | Force, endurance, intimidation | Injury |
| Clubs — Dexterity | Evasion, deception, improvisation | Stumble |
| Diamonds — Intelligence | Analysis, persuasion, planning | Stress |
| Hearts — Weird | Intuition, empathy, invocation | Curse |

Choose a primary suit and a different secondary suit. From **one deck**, take **2, 4, 6, 8 of every suit**, add **A, 5, 9 of your primary**, and add **3, 7 of your secondary**. Shuffle these **21 cards**: seven primary, six secondary, four of each other suit, with no statuses. These choices only determine starting cards, never which approach you may declare. The remaining cards and the entire second deck form your reserve, **every 10 among them**: nobody starts holding a 10, but they are gained and evolved into like any other card. Removed cards return there; gained cards come from there. Keep at least 13 ordinary cards.

## Before drawing

The GM sets **N from 1–13 tricks** to fit the situation, checks survival, and states goal costs and consequences. A typical check runs **2p + 1 tricks for p players**; use **1 trick** for a quick check. Each player commits to **one separate goal** and declares an approach whose suit is their **personal trump**. Your approach must fit the fiction — being good at sneaking does not help once you have been spotted — and the GM has final say on whether one applies. The GM declares the world's approach and trump. Goals, approaches, and trumps stay fixed for the check. The world is the first leader unless the GM specifies someone else. Only your wins score your goal, even after meeting its cost; help others through fiction and card play.

An immediate threat can **force your goal**, such as getting through an attack safely. You still choose your approach. Combine simultaneous threats into one goal before drawing.

Draw N cards. **Once drawing begins, no tactical deliberation, card advice, or signals.** Narration, rules questions, and effect declarations are welcome; other suits can represent supporting actions within your approach.

## Every trick

1. Everyone plays **face-up in turn order**, starting with the leader and going clockwise, the world at the GM's seat. You see every card played before yours. **Follow the led suit if able.** The world plays its leftmost legal card.
2. A card is trump if its suit matches **its owner's personal trump** and its owner **could not follow the led suit** — this holds for the world too. The **highest trump** takes the trick; with no trump played, the **highest card of any suit** takes it. The leader never plays trump, having chosen the suit, so leading is the weaker seat. When you cannot follow, choose: spend your trump to take the trick, or play another suit and save it.
3. Score **one trick for the winner's goal**; the world scores nothing for players. Break a tie for highest by **led suit** first, then by the **earliest card played**.
4. Resolve status effects, then optional consolations. Keep played cards in a row until cleanup unless removed. The winner leads next; if anyone played Stumble, the world leads instead. Play all N tricks.

## When you lose a trick

You may use your card's **printed suit** consolation, even if an ally won. Resolve independently while everyone else resolves theirs; finish before the next lead. After the final trick, only Revelation's question is available.

| Consolation | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Brace — Spades** | On your next play, add or subtract **this card's printed value** to or from the card you play, minimum 0. Choose which as you play the card. |
| **Reposition — Clubs** | You may ignore follow-suit on your next play, which **counts as being unable to follow**, so your personal trump can win. |
| **Recalculate — Diamonds** | Discard one card from hand, then draw one. If that draw reshuffles your deck, you may evolve. |
| **Revelation — Hearts** | Ask the GM one question about the situation for a brief truthful answer, **or** put a status from discard atop your deck; your next card takes that status's suit instead of its own. |

Next-play effects expire after that play or at check end. Brace changes value, not suit priority, and **Braces never stack** — you hold at most one, and a new one replaces it. For Revelation's next play, treat cards in hand as the chosen suit for follow-suit, leading, and trump; consolation uses the printed suit. Your whole hand takes that suit, so you follow if it matches the led suit and cannot follow if it does not — picking your own trump suit when the led suit differs wins you the trick. Your personal trump never changes.

## Statuses

Gain statuses into discard; if none remain in reserve, gain no copy.

- **Injury:** zero-value Spades. Gain only at cleanup or between checks, then scan for survival; consequences during play wait for cleanup.
- **Stumble:** if anyone plays it, the world leads the next trick, regardless of who won.
- **Stress:** whenever drawn, gain another Stress into discard, including on redraws and during play.
- **Curse:** your Curses share one supernatural compulsion; each violation gains one Stress for every Curse **not in your reserve** — six minus the number still sitting there. Remove Curses through evolution, never rest; removing the last ends the compulsion.

## Blessed

Your four Jokers are **Blessed** cards, granted by the GM for play at the table — recapping last session, sharp roleplaying — never for something your character did. A granted Blessed goes on **top of your deck**, so you draw it next.

**Blessed wins any trick it is played in**, beating trump and everything else. It has no suit: it never follows suit, and leading it sets no led suit, so everyone may play anything. Two in one trick go to the earliest played. It is not a status, so rest and evolution never touch it, and a Brace expires unused on it.

A **played** Blessed returns to reserve, so it never reaches discard or a survival scan. An **unplayed** one cleans up like an ordinary card and cycles back around; hold one by simply not playing it.

**Between tricks you may give your Blessed to another player, then draw one card.** Handing it over is the only signal the no-coordination rule allows — no discussion or advice with it.

## Evolution and rest

When you must draw from an empty deck, shuffle discard. **You may evolve once per shuffle**, in preparation or mid-check: choose two matching statuses in discard and return one to reserve; the other remains in discard. Then either **draw three ordinary cards of that suit at random from reserve, keep one in discard, and return the rest**, or return **one ordinary card of that suit** from discard to reserve. Draw fewer if unavailable. During play, set both matching statuses aside before shuffling; after the trick, return one to reserve and put the other on top of discard, then resolve the rest. Evolution removes exactly one status.

At a GM-granted rest, return any non-Curse statuses from discard to reserve. How often rest comes is the GM's main dial for lethality.

## Finish and survive

A goal succeeds if its score meets its cost. Among incompatible goals that meet their costs, the **greatest margin over cost** wins. A tie gives the agreed stalemate.

Resolve goals and deaths. Shuffle unplayed cards together and discard them, then discard your played row earliest to latest, skipping removed cards. Add consequences with **Injuries last**. Never rearrange discard.

Living characters count Injuries in their top N discard cards, or all if fewer exist. **Two or more force survival as your goal, costing that count. Failure means death.** Survival takes priority over other goals; lock its cost and do not duplicate a pending requirement.

| Scan trigger | N | Survival applies |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Check start, before drawing or reshuffling | Upcoming check's size | This check |
| Injury gained at cleanup or between checks | Last completed check's size | Immediately |

Gain-triggered checks use the scan's N, with **no rest beforehand**. Other discard changes wait for the next start scan. Success leaves Injuries, so later scans may require survival again; rest may clear them before an ordinary check.
