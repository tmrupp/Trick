Set up a check

  1. Agree goals, targets and stakes. Pick each target from page 2.
  2. Declare an approach. Its suit is your trump for the check.
  3. Size it:
    CheckTricksTargets
    Standard3 per player, +13–20, default 10
    Quick2 per player, +13–10, default 5
    Solo63–10, default 6
  4. Deal the world one card per trick from the GM's shuffled A–10 deck, face-up in a row. Everyone draws the same number.

Play a trick

  1. The world leads the next card in its row.
  2. Everyone chooses a card face-down, then all reveal together. No talk about plays.
  3. Find the single highest card on the table, including the world card. Count any Brace.
  4. If that card is a player's, in the world card's suit or its owner's trump, it wins: its owner scores the world card's value.
  5. Otherwise the world takes the trick: the world card is highest, the highest card is off-suit, or the top cards tie.
  6. Everyone who didn't win takes their card's consolation, all at once.
Winning isn't optional. If your card is highest and on-suit, you take the world card, even if it busts you. To stay safe, play low, or let someone play over you.
High off-suit cards block. They can't win, but nobody beats them, so the world takes the trick. Use one to stop an ally busting.
Suits: Strength ♥ · Dexterity ♠ · Intelligence ♦ · Weird ♣. Ace = 1. J, Q and K are statuses, worth 0.

Consolations

From your losing card's printed suit, statuses included. Brace and Mirror apply to your next play only: turn the losing card sideways as a reminder. No consolations after the final trick.

Brace
Strength ♥
Your next card counts this card's value higher for beating.
Reach: a small card can take a big world card.
Slip
Dexterity ♠
Return this card to your hand. You lost the trick, but spent nothing.

Recalculate

Discard one card from hand, then draw one.

Weird ♣

Invert the prize: a world 2 scores 9, a world 10 scores 1.

Example

Target 10. Ana (Strength trump) is on 2 and needs exactly 8. The world leads 6♦, and an 8♠ is next, but her best Heart is a 5. She plays 4♥, which loses, so she Braces for 4. Next trick she plays 5♥: it counts 5 + 4 = 9 for beating, takes the 8♠, and she scores the world card's 8: exactly 10. For the rest of the check she plays low.

Statuses, special cases and targets

Statuses

Statuses are ordinary cards worth 0. They follow every rule, and their printed suit gives its consolation as usual.

StatusWhen it losesIts own rule
InjuryBrace for 0, which does nothing.Two or more Injuries in your recent discard make survival a forced goal.
StumbleSlips back to your hand.Price: when you play Stumble, discard an ordinary card from hand, your choice.
StressRecalculate. You may discard another status.Whenever you draw Stress, gain another Stress into discard.
CurseMirrors your next win.Breaking the compulsion gains one Stress per Curse outside your reserve.
  • A Braced status can win: a 0 Braced by 6 beats a world 5, and you score the 5.
  • Once you're on target, a status is a handy pass. Early on, it's a dead slot.
  • Bust: gain your trump suit's status (Strength → Injury, Dexterity → Stumble, Intelligence → Stress, Weird → Curse). A Curse names its compulsion after what went wrong.
  • 4–6 short: the complication may be a status. Critical: shed a non-Curse status.

Special cases

  • Blessed (joker) always takes the trick and scores the world card. Two Blessed tie, so the world takes it. A played Blessed returns to reserve.
  • Survival is a forced goal with target 2 per Injury counted. A bust, or 7+ short, is death.
  • Incompatible goals: the better result wins; on the same step, the tally closer to its target. Equal results produce the agreed stalemate.
  • Empty hand: sit out the trick. Empty deck: shuffle your discard into a new one.

Playing well

  • Pick your prizes. Find world cards in the row that add up to your target.
  • Yield. If an ally needs this world card, play low. You still get a consolation.
  • Block. If this world card would bust an ally, play over them, even off-suit.
  • Set up. Brace to reach a big world card; Mirror to turn a small one big; Slip to wait.

Starter deck

Each player brings two standard decks. From one deck, take 2, 4, 6, 8 of every suit, add A, 5, 9 of your primary suit and 3, 7 of a different secondary suit: 21 cards. Everything else, including jokers, is your reserve.
