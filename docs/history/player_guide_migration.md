# Player-guide migration

Current references: [player guide](../../PLAYER_GUIDE.md), [full rules](../../RULES.md), and [item conventions](../../items/README.md). Original sources are preserved in `before_player_guide/`.

The migration preserves the newer shared-file decisions: survival scans at check start and Injury gain; competing goals compare excess over cost. Evolution samples three reserve cards and keeps one.

The following optional supplement changes are mechanical translations, not just wording updates:

- **Dying Ring:** replaces its direct survival trigger and individually marked Curses with three ordinary Curses and two gained Injuries at cleanup. Those gains use the normal survival scan. Its hand replacement uses N rather than seven.
- **Hearthhide Coat:** setting aside Injuries changes the hand but never lowers the recorded rescue cost. Returned Injuries are card movement, not newly gained statuses.
- **Wake Lantern:** its extra hand uses N cards from personal reserve, follows the world rule, counts toward trump, and contributes to the owner's fixed goal.
- **Hollow Blindfold:** an off-suit win fulfills its goal's cost rather than ending everyone's check. Its price becomes a return-safely goal in the next shared check.
- **Borrowed Face:** an inverted trick uses the lowest eligible trump or led-suit value, with normal ties.
- **Halved Locket:** complete personal pools and their Curse taboo exchange between checks, avoiding mid-hand goal/ownership changes.
- **Varn:** bargains are agreed before drawing. **Rootborn:** growth replaces optional evolution rather than nonexistent free recovery. **Moon-Eyed:** Injury removal is an explicit preparation-only ability exception.

Suit-specific item/race checks now refer to agreed goal applicability. Extra cards never add tricks. These supplements have been checked for text consistency and rendering, not playtested for balance.
