Optional peoples of Black Valley

Use with the current rules. These setting abilities are optional additions, not part of the condensed player guide.

Shared timing

Agree on abilities and any goal requirements before drawing. Each player has a separate goal and declares an approach that fixes their personal trump for the check. Wins cannot be pooled or transferred. Use choices independently during resolution; no new negotiation. Extra cards do not add tricks. Stress duplicates whenever drawn, including extra draws and redraws. All card gains use your reserve, removals return there, and survival costs stay fixed. Follow the ordinary cleanup order for extra cards.

Varn — Binding bargain

Before drawing, agree on your bargain and the compulsion it would establish if you have no Curse. Once per check, before a lead, you may gain one Curse, shuffle and discard your hand, and draw the same number of cards. No renegotiation after seeing your hand.

Moon-Eyed — Wax resilience

Before a preparation reshuffle, after any evolution, you may return one Injury from discard to reserve. This is an ability exception; ordinary reshuffling removes nothing. Once per check, before a lead, you may discard one card from hand and draw one.

Tarrans — Ichor tolerance

Your Weird cards can follow any led suit. This ability does not change their suit for leading or winning, or change your personal trump. Other cards follow suit normally. You can drink raw ichor without its ordinary craving.

Hollow-Skinned — Feeding curse

When you gain an Injury while another Injury is already in your discard, gain one Curse from reserve. If this is your first Curse, its compulsion concerns your hunger; existing Curses keep their shared compulsion. While you carry a Curse, draw two extra cards after the deal if your goal was agreed to involve force. While wearing a borrowed body outside your primal form, you also gain that person's ability; agree what this means before drawing.

Rootborn — Rebalancing growth

Before a preparation reshuffle, you may forgo your one evolution to add one random ordinary reserve card of your least-represented suit to discard. Count your ordinary cards across draw pile, hand, and discard; choose among tied suits. If none is available, add nothing. This replaces the obsolete exchange of free recovery for growth.

Ashcrik — Reed and ember

In reed-form, draw one extra card after the deal if your goal involves evasion or deception. In ember-form, do so if your goal involves force or endurance. To switch, declare a fast before a check and carry it across two reshuffles, gaining one Stumble at each. Switch after the second. Interrupting the fast cancels the switch and adds one Stress.
